How it works

  1. DLL Details. Pick the English or Filipino template, then fill in the header rows and signatories once.
  2. Lesson Content. Each box is one cell of the DLL. Type in it, or copy from Word, Google Docs or any website and paste. Bold, italics, underline, bulleted and numbered lists, and tables are kept.
  3. Import from a File. Already have the lesson in a Word file or another format? Drop the .docx or paste the whole thing. The generator matches each part by its heading and fills the cells for you. Check every cell afterwards.
  4. Preview & Download. See the filled DLL, then download it as a Word file. Several sessions download as one file, each starting on a new page.
  5. Saved DLLs. Your draft stays in this browser between visits. Export a project file to move it to another computer.

About the format

  1. The labels, green section bands, fonts (Century Gothic) and column widths come straight from the school's templates and cannot be changed here.
  2. Rows have no fixed height. A row grows as tall as its content, and long sections continue onto the next page.
  3. Pictures pasted into a cell are left out. Insert them in Word after downloading.
  4. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Everything is built inside your browser.
